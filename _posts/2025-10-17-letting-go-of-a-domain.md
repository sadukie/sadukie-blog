Long ago, I owned a domain about being a coding geekette. However, the branding with the name just never did stick for me as much as Sadukie does. So I let that domain lapse. I've had a few friends reach out to see if I had let it lapse, so I figured I better make this post.

😱

True confessions from a former domain hoarder - I let domains lapse and don't care if they get picked up from squatters. I've come down in numbers in my domains significantly.

I have no problem with letting them go.

The fact is that if I'm not attached to a brand, then it's time to move on. No regrets.
