While I was at CodeMash 2026, I had someone come up to me and tell me that the Cleveland tech community is dead. I knew that they were either not from around here or not connected to the right people. (And funny that they tell _me_ this? 🤔) So let's set some things straight.

## Why did you stop doing Cleveland Tech Events?

So there were many reasons why I stopped this...

- I had family to tend to. My family is more important to me than dealing with commmunity drama. Hands down - I DO NOT LIKE DRAMA. So I will walk away to spend my time in better ways. No regrets.
- I got tired of that guy in the audience who didn't like how I was doing things. This is the reality of community work. There are people who appreciate you, and then there are those who just want to complain about everything. There are some with excellent suggestions. But also, they realize that I'm human and can only do so much. I got tired of the complaints. See the first point about not liking drama.

## Conclusion

Cleveland Tech isn't dead. It's still as spread out as it was when I started Cleveland Tech Events in the 00s. You just have to know where to look. Or... be connected with someone in the know.
