Tonight, after talking with my boys about the Gen Alpha lingo, I threw this prompt at Gemini:

```text
Ok... so I need to use these words more often - Skibidi, GYAT, Riz, Ohio POGS, cap. Can you write a poem that explains these terms to a Xennial?
```

I think it did really well, but I'm a Xennial. So I understand it but I wasn't sure if it was true.

```text
Let me craft a rhyme to bridge the lingo gap,
For Xennials lost in the Gen Alpha rap.

Forget grunge and slap bracelets,
it's a new kind of cool,
Where “sick” means awesome
and following rules is drool.

First up, there's skibidi, a sound with no exact translation,
But if you see someone bust a move with pure elation,
That's skibidi in action, a sight to behold,
Stylish and smooth, a story untold.

Then there's GYAT, whispered with a heart full of glee,
An acronym for “God You're Attractive,” don't you see?
A compliment finer than anything flannel can bring,
So next time someone says GYAT, your heart might take wing.

Riz is the magic, the charm, the flirtatious art,
The ability to win someone's captivated heart.
But beware of Ohio riz, a social faux pas so grand,
It means your flirting's lame, like socks with sandals in the sand.

POGS may sound like a forgotten game from your youth,
But Gen Alpha uses it for a different kind of truth.
A shout of excitement, a celebration of sorts,
Like finding a pristine copy of Nirvana's “Nevermind” reports.

And finally, cap, a word so very plain,
But it means “no lie,” dispelling any doubt or strain.
So listen up Xennials, put down the scrunchies for a bit,
These terms are the key to understanding the next generation's hit.

- Google Gemini
```

I asked my boys if this was “bussin'” and they said “bet”. 😂

I just want some insight on their words. Thanks, Gemini!
