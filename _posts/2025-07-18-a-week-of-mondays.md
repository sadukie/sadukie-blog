This has been a week of Mondays.

Knowing that a friend is separated from his wife and young sons, kept in an ICE facility, while they navigate the chaos of his immigration status. While we can help them [raise funds](https://www.gofundme.com/f/help-bring-rami-home-to-his-family), it's still hard knowing they are apart. Rami was one of my favorite guys to talk with on the IT admin side of things - from general geeking out to getting really good advice on some tough things. When we had a horrible situation happen in the office, Rami was the one I talked with because I felt horrible and knew he would have the advice I needed, even if it wasn't what I wanted to hear. He always had my back. I'm worried for him.

The smell of death - suspecting a mouse that we can't find. The markings on the trap indicate a struggle. We can't smell it in the house. But it's strong right outside the front door.

Migraines.😖

Something (maybe hawk? maybe the cat itself?) leaving a carcass and its fur all over the front lawn. Stronger smells of death. 🤢

35-year anniversary of the passing of my grandmother, who helped raise me in my early days. 🥰

Tracking down issues in my code only to find that one of them was off by a character - a `/`. 😒

ADHD battles with one of the kids. Gotta love when the mornings start with emotional dysregulation, yelling, false accusations, and fighting. 😵

If you see me today and see me downhearted, frustrated, and not my normal positive self, know that it's been a week.

And yes - trash pickup happened and the smell of death is gone. 🗑️

I made some exciting breakthroughs in code that I was fighting with. 👩🏻‍💻

I got done with conference session review and am excited to see the final line-up - holy cow!

But honestly? This week is just sitting heavy on me.

I promise you - it isn't all sunshine and rainbows here. Not even [pink fluffy unicorns dancing on rainbows](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWM2joNb9NE).

Hopefully things get better soon.
