I was listening to this song recently, and between this and talking with friends on the job hunt, I've been thinking. Something you need to realize is that **you own your career**.

## Other People's Visions

Other people will have visions of you. They'll see you speak on a topic and think you can solve their problems. They've got use cases where they can apply your experience. However, their visions may not always line-up with yours.

I have a company that has been watching me throughout the career, wanting to collaborate with me at the right time. I know about them, as we cross paths at networking events and other tech community adventures. The time though has not come for us to collaborate - mostly because their industry is one I avoid intentionally.
