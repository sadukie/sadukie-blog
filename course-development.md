Since early 2010s, I’ve been able to include course development as part of my skillset. I had a great exposure to learning designers and other specialists in that area, which has helped shape who I am.

If you want to work with me in course design, you need to understand the following:

- I come from the background of [modular course design](https://ctl.wiley.com/modular-course-design/) and [backwards design](https://cft.vanderbilt.edu/guides-sub-pages/understanding-by-design/).  Basically, I see building blocks (modules) and start with the end in mind.
- I’m a big fan of [I do, We do, You do](https://www.evidencebasedteaching.org.au/the-i-do-we-do-you-do-model-explained/).
